# Chapter 29: Growth Hormone, IGF-1, Somatostatin, and Pubertal Physiology (生长激素、IGF-1与青春期发育生理学)

## Overview & Syllabus Structure
This chapter comprises **150 board-style USMLE Step 1 questions** meticulously engineered according to Kaplan Medical Physiology and First Aid 2025 guidelines. Designed for mastery of the somatotropic axis, growth plate chondrogenesis, metabolic counter-regulation, pubertal neuroendocrinology, and disorders of growth and sexual maturation.

### Structure:
- **Block 1 (Questions 001-050)**: Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Somatotropic Axis, GHRH (Gs-cAMP-PKA), Somatostatin (Gi-cAMP/GIRK), Ghrelin (GHSR-1a), Pulsatile GH Secretion during Slow-Wave Sleep, Dynamic Provocative Testing (OGTT, ITT, Arginine, Glucagon), GHR Dimerization, and JAK2-STAT5b Signaling.
- **Block 2 (Questions 051-100)**: IGF-1 Biology, IGF-1R RTK Signaling (PI3K-Akt vs MAPK), IGF-2 Decoy Receptor, IGFBPs and Ternary Complexes, Epiphyseal Zonal Histology, Dual Effector Model, Estrogen-Driven Epiphyseal Fusion (ER-alpha/Aromatase Deficiency), Achondroplasia, and Metabolic Divergence.
- **Block 3 (Questions 101-150)**: Pubertal Physiology: Adrenarche vs Gonadarche, KNDy Neurons (Kisspeptin), Leptin Energy Sensing, Tanner Staging Chronology, Precocious Puberty (Central vs Peripheral, McCune-Albright, Testotoxicosis), Delayed Puberty (Turner, Klinefelter, CDGP, Kallmann Syndrome), and Pituitary Lesions.
