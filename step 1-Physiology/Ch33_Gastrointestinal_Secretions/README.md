# Chapter 33: Gastrointestinal Secretions (消化道分泌生理学)

## Overview & Syllabus Structure
This chapter comprises **150 board-style USMLE Step 1 questions** meticulously engineered according to Kaplan Medical Physiology and First Aid 2025 guidelines. Designed for mastery of salivary gland physiology and ductal modification, gastric mucosal architecture, molecular biophysics of parietal cell hydrochloric acid secretion and the alkaline tide, neurohormonal regulation of acid secretion, peptic ulcer disease and pharmacology, pancreatic exocrine zymogens and enteropeptidase activation cascade, ductal bicarbonate secretion and CFTR biophysics, cystic fibrosis, acute and chronic pancreatitis, biliary tract physiology, bile acid synthesis, enterohepatic circulation, gallstone pathogenesis, bilirubin metabolism, inherited hyperbilirubinemias, intestinal crypt secretion and diarrheal biophysics, stool osmotic gap, and oral rehydration therapy.
