# Chapter 28: Thyroid Hormone Physiology (甲状腺激素生理学)

## Overview & Syllabus Structure
This chapter comprises **150 board-style USMLE Step 1 questions** meticulously engineered according to Kaplan Medical Physiology and First Aid 2025 guidelines. Designed for mastery of thyroid follicular hormonogenesis, plasma protein binding, peripheral deiodinase regulation, nuclear receptor genomics, metabolic calorigenesis, cardiovascular hemodynamics, and endocrine emergencies.

### Structure:
- **Block 1 (Questions 001-050)**: Follicular Architecture, NIS Trapping, Pendrin, Thyroglobulin Synthesis, TPO Oxidation/Organification/Coupling, Colloid Storage, Lysosomal Hydrolysis, DEHAL1 Recycling, and HPT Axis Control.
- **Block 2 (Questions 051-100)**: Plasma Binding Proteins (TBG, Transthyretin, Albumin), Free Hormone Kinetics, Deiodinase Selenoenzymes (D1, D2, D3), MCT8 Transporter, Nuclear Receptors (TR-alpha, TR-beta, RXR), Resistance to Thyroid Hormone, BMR/Calorigenesis, and Cardiovascular Hemodynamics.
- **Block 3 (Questions 101-150)**: Clinical Pathophysiology: Graves Disease, Toxic MNG, Toxic Adenoma, Subacute Thyroiditis (de Quervain vs Silent), Factitious Thyrotoxicosis, Hashimoto Thyroiditis, Riedel Thyroiditis, Thyroid Storm Emergency Protocol, Myxedema Coma, Thionamides, and Pregnancy Management.
