# Chapter 30: Male Reproductive Physiology (男性生殖生理学)

## Overview & Syllabus Structure
This chapter comprises **150 board-style USMLE Step 1 questions** meticulously engineered according to Kaplan Medical Physiology and First Aid 2025 guidelines. Designed for mastery of testicular architecture, spermatogenesis, Leydig and Sertoli cell endocrinology, androgen metabolism, semen analysis, autonomic neurovascular erectile function, and disorders of sexual development.

### Structure:
- **Block 1 (Questions 001-050)**: Testicular Architecture, Sertoli Cells (Blood-Testis Barrier, ABP, Inhibin B, AMH), Leydig Cells (LH Receptor, StAR, Steroidogenesis), HPT Negative Feedback, Scrotal Thermoregulation, Spermatogenesis Timeline, and Autonomic Control of Erection/Emission/Ejaculation.
- **Block 2 (Questions 051-100)**: Primary vs Secondary Hypogonadism, Klinefelter Syndrome, Mumps Orchitis, Chemotherapy Toxicity, Semen Analysis (WHO Reference Standards), Obstructive vs Non-Obstructive Azoospermia, Y-Chromosome AZF Microdeletions, TRT Pharmacology, and Gynecomastia.
- **Block 3 (Questions 101-150)**: 46,XY DSD (CAIS, 5-alpha-Reductase Deficiency, 17-beta-HSD3, StAR Deficiency), Multi-Tier Erectile Dysfunction Management (PDE5 Inhibitors, Alprostadil, Trimix), BPH (Alpha-Blockers vs 5-ARIs, TURP Syndrome), Prostate Cancer ADT, and Penile Disorders (Peyronie, Fracture).
