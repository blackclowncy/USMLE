# Chapter 34: Gastrointestinal Digestion & Absorption (消化与吸收生理学)

## Overview & Syllabus Structure
This chapter comprises **150 board-style USMLE Step 1 questions** meticulously engineered according to Kaplan Medical Physiology and First Aid 2025 guidelines. Designed for mastery of carbohydrate luminal digestion (salivary/pancreatic alpha-amylase) and brush-border disaccharidases (sucrase-isomaltase, lactase-phlorizin hydrolase), hexose membrane transport biophysics (SGLT1 secondary active transport, GLUT5 facilitated diffusion, GLUT2 basolateral exit), carbohydrate malabsorption (lactose intolerance, fructose malabsorption, congenital glucose-galactose malabsorption, sucrase-isomaltase deficiency), small intestinal mucosal diseases (celiac disease, dermatitis herpetiformis, tropical sprue, Whipple disease), gastric and pancreatic protein hydrolysis (pepsinogen kinetics, enteropeptidase zymogen cascade, endopeptidases vs exopeptidases), apical oligopeptide cotransport (PEPT1 H+-coupled tertiary active symport) and amino acid transport disorders (Hartnup disease, cystinuria), dietary lipid emulsification, pancreatic lipase/colipase kinetics, mixed micelle diffusion across the unstirred water layer, enterocyte re-esterification, chylomicron packaging by MTTP and ApoB-48, abetalipoproteinemia, medium-chain triglyceride portal bypass, enteric hyperoxaluria, short bowel syndrome, bile acid diarrhea, fat-soluble vitamin assimilation and deficiencies (vitamins A, D, E, K), water-soluble vitamin assimilation (thiamine, riboflavin, niacin, pyridoxine, folate vs cobalamin, intrinsic factor and terminal ileal cubam receptor, subacute combined degeneration, Schilling test, vitamin C), duodenal mineral transport (iron DMT1, ferroportin-1, hepcidin regulation, hemochromatosis; calcium TRPV6, calbindin-D9k, calcitriol; magnesium TRPM6 and PPI hypomagnesemia; zinc ZIP4 acrodermatitis enteropathica; copper CTR1/ATP7A Menkes vs ATP7B Wilson disease), 9-liter gastrointestinal fluid turnover, and master anatomical nutrient absorptive mapping.
