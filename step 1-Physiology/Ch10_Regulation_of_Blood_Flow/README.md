# Chapter 10: Regulation of Blood Flow (局部血流调节、器官微循环与运动生理学)

## 📌 Chapter Overview & Blueprint (Kaplan Medical Physiology 2023, pp. 114–125)
This chapter systematically establishes the fundamental biophysical, microcirculatory, neural, and metabolic mechanisms that match regional blood flow to tissue metabolic demand. It covers:
1. **The Fick Principle of Blood Flow Determination**:
   - Master governing formula: $\\text{Blood Flow} (Q) = \\frac{\\text{Rate of Oxygen Consumption } (VO_2)}{C_aO_2 - C_vO_2}$.
   - Requirement of pulmonary artery sampling for true mixed venous oxygen ($C_vO_2$).
   - Oxygen content formula: $C_aO_2 = (1.34 \\times [Hb] \\times SaO_2) + (0.003 \\times PaO_2)$.
2. **Capillary Microcirculation and Starling Forces**:
   - Net fluid filtration equation: $J_v = K_f [(P_c - P_i) - \\sigma (\\pi_c - \\pi_i)]$.
   - Capillary hydrostatic pressure ($P_c$) axial gradient and the precapillary-to-postcapillary resistance ratio ($R_v / R_a$).
   - Reflection coefficient ($\\sigma$) collapse in burns and sepsis; lymphatic safety factors against edema.
   - Precapillary sphincters and functional capillary recruitment.
3. **General Mechanisms of Organ Autoregulation**:
   - Autoregulatory pressure plateau: maintaining constant flow across fluctuating arterial pressures.
   - **Myogenic Mechanism (Bayliss effect)**: Stretch-activated cation channels, depolarization, L-type $Ca^{2+}$ entry, and active vasoconstriction.
   - **Metabolic Hypothesis**: Inadequate flow allows local vasodilator metabolites to accumulate, relaxing smooth muscle.
   - **Active (Functional) Hyperemia** vs. **Reactive Hyperemia** (repayment of metabolic debt).
   - Endothelial mediators: eNOS, Nitric Oxide, cGMP/PKG, Prostacyclin ($PGI_2$), EDHF, Endothelin-1.
4. **Organ-Specific Circulatory Beds**:
   - **Coronary Circulation**: Dominated by local metabolic autoregulation (Adenosine and NO); near-maximal resting oxygen extraction (~75-80%); left ventricular flow restricted to diastole due to systolic extravascular compression.
   - **Cerebral Circulation**: Autoregulated tightly between MAP 60 to 140 mm Hg; exquisitely sensitive to arterial $PCO_2$ ($PaCO_2$); severe hypoxemia threshold ($PaO_2 < 50$ mm Hg); neurovascular coupling (astrocytic end-feet); Monro-Kellie doctrine ($CPP = MAP - ICP$).
   - **Renal Circulation**: High perfusion (20-25% CO) with low extraction (10-15%); autoregulated via myogenic tone and tubuloglomerular feedback (macula densa NKCC2 sensing luminal NaCl delivery); outer medullary thick ascending limb hypoxic vulnerability.
   - **Pulmonary Circulation**: Low resistance, high compliance system; West zones 1, 2, and 3; unique **Hypoxic Pulmonary Vasoconstriction (HPV)** matching ventilation to perfusion ($V/Q$); capillary recruitment/distension reducing PVR in exercise; HAPE.
   - **Cutaneous Circulation**: Controlled by sympathetic thermoregulation (anterior hypothalamus); apical arteriovenous anastomoses (AVAs) with adrenergic alpha-1 tone vs. non-apical sympathetic cholinergic active vasodilation and sweating.
   - **Splanchnic & Hepatic Circulation**: Splanchnic venous reservoir; dual hepatic supply (Portal vein 75% flow / 50% O2 vs. Hepatic artery 25% flow / 50% O2); Hepatic Arterial Buffer Response (HABR); postprandial hyperemia.
5. **The Fetal Circulation and Birth Transitions**:
   - Specialized shunts: Umbilical vein ($PO_2 \\approx 30-35$ mm Hg), Ductus Venosus (bypassing liver to IVC), Foramen Ovale (preferential stream to left heart and brain), Ductus Arteriosus (bypassing high-resistance lungs).
   - Circulatory transition at first breath: Lung expansion and oxygenation collapse PVR; cord clamping doubles SVR; LAP rises above RAP, closing the foramen ovale; oxygen surge and $PGE_2$ withdrawal constrict the ductus arteriosus.
   - Pharmacological modulation: Indomethacin closes PDA; Alprostadil ($PGE_1$) maintains PDA patency in ductal-dependent congenital heart lesions.
   - Anatomical remnants: Ligamentum teres hepatis, ligamentum venosum, ligamentum arteriosum, medial umbilical ligaments.
6. **Cardiovascular Stress & Exercise Physiology**:
   - Central command + exercise pressor reflex (mechanoreceptors and metaboreceptors).
   - Dynamic exercise: HR $\uparrow$, SV $\uparrow$ (plateaus at 40-50% VO2 max), CO $\uparrow$ up to 4-5x; massive skeletal muscle vasodilation (functional sympatholysis) collapsing systemic TPR; systolic BP $\uparrow$, diastolic BP flat/$\downarrow$, pulse pressure widens; coronary flow $\uparrow$; renal/splanchnic flow $\downarrow$; cerebral flow constant; $SvO_2$ drops to <25%; rightward Bohr shift.
   - Static (isometric) exercise: Mechanical vessel compression triggers massive pressor reflex, spiking diastolic BP.
   - Environmental extremes: High altitude acclimatization, spaceflight microgravity fluid shifts, $+G_z$ acceleration blackout, diving decompression sickness.
