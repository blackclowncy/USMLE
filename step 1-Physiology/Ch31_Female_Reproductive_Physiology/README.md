# Chapter 31: Female Reproductive Physiology (女性生殖生理学)

## Overview & Syllabus Structure
This chapter comprises **150 board-style USMLE Step 1 questions** meticulously engineered according to Kaplan Medical Physiology and First Aid 2025 guidelines. Designed for mastery of oogenesis, folliculogenesis, the two-cell two-gonadotropin steroidogenic model, the menstrual cycle (follicular and luteal phases, LH surge switch), amenorrhea workup (primary and secondary), PCOS neuroendocrinology, menopause, contraception pharmacology, the fetoplacental unit, parturition, and lactation physiology.

### Structure:
- **Block 1 (Questions 001-050)**: Oogenesis & Meiotic Arrest (Dictyotene Prophase I to Metaphase II), Folliculogenesis & AMH, Two-Cell Two-Gonadotropin Model (Theca LH vs Granulosa FSH), Follicular Phase & Proliferative Endometrium, Cervical Mucus Dynamics, Estrogen Positive Feedback Switch & LH Surge, Corpus Luteum Function, Secretory Phase, and Luteolysis.
- **Block 2 (Questions 051-100)**: Primary Amenorrhea Differential (Turner Syndrome, MRKH Mullerian Agenesis, CAIS, Swyer Syndrome, 5-alpha-Reductase Deficiency), Secondary Amenorrhea (Functional Hypothalamic Amenorrhea, Prolactinoma, Asherman Syndrome, POI), Progesterone Challenge Test, PCOS Pathophysiology & Rotterdam Criteria, Menopause & Vasomotor Instability, HRT Risks/Benefits, and Contraceptive Pharmacology (Combined OCPs, Progestin-Only, Copper IUD, Emergency Contraception).
- **Block 3 (Questions 101-150)**: Maternal Adaptations to Pregnancy (Cardiovascular, Respiratory, Renal), Fetoplacental Unit Steroidogenesis (hCG, Placental Progesterone, Estriol Synthesis requiring Fetal Adrenal/Liver DHEA-S), hPL & Gestational Diabetes, Preeclampsia & Spiral Artery Remodeling Failure, Parturition (Estrogen/Progesterone Ratio, Oxytocin, PGF2a, Ferguson Reflex, Cervical Ripening), and Lactation Physiology (Prolactin vs Oxytocin Milk Ejection Reflex, Lactational Amenorrhea).
