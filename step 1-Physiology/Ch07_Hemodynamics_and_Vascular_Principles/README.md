# Chapter 07: Hemodynamics and Vascular Principles (血流动力学基本原理与血管生理)

## 📌 Chapter Overview & Blueprint (Kaplan Medical Physiology 2023, pp. 82–89)
This chapter systematically establishes the core physical and physiological foundations of the cardiovascular system. It covers:
1. **The Systemic and Pulmonary Circulations in Series**:
   - Equal volume flow in steady state ($CO_{LV} = CO_{RV} = Q$).
   - Equivalent gas compositions across serial anatomical conduits (pulmonary veins $\approx$ systemic arteries; right atrium $\approx$ pulmonary arteries).
   - Hemodynamic consequences of disruption (left-to-right shunts, forward vs. backward heart failure).
2. **Pressure, Flow, and Vascular Resistance (Poiseuille-Hagen Equation)**:
   - Flow rate equation: $Q = \frac{\Delta P}{R} = \frac{P_1 - P_2}{R}$.
   - Resistance formula: $R = \frac{8 \eta L}{\pi r^4} \propto \frac{\eta L}{r^4}$.
   - Fourth-power radius sensitivity ($r^4$): dramatic impact of vasodilation/vasoconstriction on regional organ perfusion.
   - Upstream vs. downstream pressure shifts across pre-capillary vs. post-capillary resistances.
   - Arterioles as the primary site of systemic vascular resistance (SVR/TPR) and steepest pressure drop.
3. **Blood Viscosity ($\eta$) and Hematocrit Dynamics**:
   - Hematocrit as the prime determinant of whole blood viscosity; non-Newtonian exponential rise above 55%.
   - Clinical polycythemia (Polycythemia vera, high altitude acclimatization, EPO doping, relative hemoconcentration) vs. severe chronic anemia.
   - Hyperviscosity syndromes (Waldenström macroglobulinemia IgM pentamers, Multiple Myeloma rouleaux formation).
   - Microvascular rheology: Fåhræus-Lindqvist effect, sickle cell HbS polymerization, and hereditary spherocytosis.
4. **Velocity of Blood Flow and Cross-Sectional Area**:
   - Linear velocity definition: $v = \frac{Q}{A}$.
   - Minimal linear velocity in systemic capillaries ($v \approx 0.03$ cm/s) optimizing diffusive solute and gas exchange.
   - Maximum linear velocity in the ascending aorta ($v \approx 100$ cm/s) and across focal stenoses (Law of Continuity).
   - Simplified Bernoulli equation in echocardiography: $\Delta P = 4 v^2$.
5. **Laminar vs. Turbulent Blood Flow (The Reynolds Number)**:
   - Parabolic laminar flow profile with axial streaming and endothelial wall shear stress (eNOS activation).
   - Reynolds number formula: $N_R = \frac{\rho \cdot D \cdot v}{\eta}$.
   - Threshold for turbulence ($N_R > 2000-3000$); non-linear pressure-flow resistance ($\Delta P \propto Q^2$).
   - Ascending aorta operational predisposition to turbulence; flow murmurs in anemia and pregnancy.
   - Arterial bruits, Korotkoff sounds, post-stenotic dilatation, and Virchow's triad thrombogenesis.
6. **Series vs. Parallel Vascular Circuits**:
   - Additive series resistance ($R_{total} = \sum R_i$) vs. reciprocal parallel resistance ($\frac{1}{R_{total}} = \sum \frac{1}{R_i}$).
   - Advantage of parallel organ branching for independent autoregulation; TPR surge following organ resection (nephrectomy).
   - Coronary steal phenomenon across parallel myocardial beds.
7. **Vessel Compliance, Capacitance, and Wall Tension**:
   - Compliance formula: $C = \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta P}$; systemic veins as the high-compliance capacitance reservoir (unstressed volume, ~70% of blood).
   - Sympathetic $\alpha_1$-mediated venoconstriction mobilizing unstressed volume to defend preload and mean circulatory filling pressure (MCFP).
   - The aortic Windkessel effect and age-related arteriosclerosis: loss of arterial compliance causing isolated systolic hypertension and widened pulse pressure.
   - Law of Laplace for cylinders ($T = P \cdot r$, $\sigma = \frac{P \cdot r}{w}$) and spheres ($\sigma = \frac{P \cdot r}{2w}$): aortic wall tension, abdominal aortic aneurysm rupture catastrophe, Berry aneurysms, diverticulosis, aortic dissection, dilated cardiomyopathy, and adaptive concentric hypertrophy.
