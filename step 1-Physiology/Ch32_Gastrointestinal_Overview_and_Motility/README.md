# Chapter 32: Gastrointestinal Overview & Motility (胃肠总论与消化道动力学)

## Overview & Syllabus Structure
This chapter comprises **150 board-style USMLE Step 1 questions** meticulously engineered according to Kaplan Medical Physiology and First Aid 2025 guidelines. Designed for mastery of the enteric nervous system, gastrointestinal pacemakers and electrophysiology, deglutition, esophageal motility and disorders (achalasia, GERD, spasms, scleroderma), gastric motility and emptying dynamics (gastroparesis, dumping syndrome, MMC, hypertrophic pyloric stenosis), intestinal motility (segmentation vs peristalsis, SIBO, colonic haustrations and mass movements), defecation reflex, Hirschsprung disease, intestinal obstruction vs pseudo-obstruction (Ogilvie syndrome), irritable bowel syndrome, and gastrointestinal prokinetic and laxative pharmacology.

### Structure:
- **Block 1 (Questions 001-050)**: Enteric Nervous System Plexuses (Auerbach vs Meissner), Interstitial Cells of Cajal (c-kit/CD117, BER Slow Wave Gradients), Excitatory (ACh, Substance P) vs Inhibitory (VIP, NO) Neurotransmitters, Deglutition Reflex & UES, Primary vs Secondary Peristalsis, LES Receptive Relaxation, Achalasia (Pathology, Chicago Classification, Chagas Disease), GERD (tLESRs, Hiatal Hernias, Barrett Esophagus, 24-hr pH-Impedance), Esophageal Spasms (DES, Jackhammer), Systemic Sclerosis (Scleroderma Esophagus), and Esophageal Diverticula (Zenker Killian Triangle).
- **Block 2 (Questions 051-100)**: Gastric Receptive Relaxation & Fundic Accommodation (Vagovagal Reflex, VIP/NO), Post-Vagotomy Motility, Antral Trituration & Retropulsion (Sieve Function), Gastric Emptying Kinetics (Liquids vs Solids, Osmolality, Acid/Secretin, Fat/CCK), Diabetic Gastroparesis (Vagal Neuropathy, nNOS/ICC Loss, Glycemic Mismatch), Prokinetics (Metoclopramide, Erythromycin Tachyphylaxis), Early vs Late Dumping Syndrome (Incretin Surge), Migrating Motor Complex (Phases I-IV, Motilin Housekeeping Waves), Infantile Hypertrophic Pyloric Stenosis (Pathophysiology, Olive Mass, Paradoxical Aciduria, Ultrasound, Preop Resuscitation), and GI Motility Peptides (Ghrelin, PYY Ileal Brake, GLP-1, Neurotensin, VIPoma, Gastrinoma, Somatostatinoma, Glucagonoma, Carcinoid Syndrome & Heart Disease).
- **Block 3 (Questions 101-150)**: Small Intestinal Segmentation vs Peristalsis, Bayliss-Starling Law of the Intestine (Ascending ACh/Substance P vs Descending NO/VIP), Gastroileal Reflex, Ileocecal Sphincter Barrier Mechanics, Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth (SIBO, Bile Acid Deconjugation, B12/Folate Dynamics, Breath Testing), Colonic Haustral Contractions vs Mass Movements, Gastrocolic Reflex, Defecation Reflex (RAIR, IAS vs EAS, Puborectalis Sling Angle), Hirschsprung Disease (Arrest of Neural Crest Migration, RET Mutation, Spastic Aganglionic Segment, Squirt Sign, Suction Biopsy AChE/Calretinin, Absent RAIR, HAEC Enterocolitis), Mechanical SBO (Adhesions, Strangulation Cascade, Closed-Loop, Gallstone Ileus Rigler Triad, Intussusception Ultrasound Target Sign, Meckel Diverticulum), Paralytic Ileus vs Ogilvie Syndrome (Laplace's Law, Cecal Perforation Risk, IV Neostigmine), Irritable Bowel Syndrome (Rome IV, Visceral Hypersensitivity, Linaclotide GC-C Agonist, Lubiprostone ClC-2 Activator, Alosetron 5-HT3 Antagonist), and Laxative Pharmacology (Bulk, Osmotic PEG/Lactulose, Stimulant Senna/Melanosis Coli, Docusate, PAMORAs Naloxegol, Pelvic Floor Dyssynergia, Volvulus Sigmoid vs Cecal, Toxic Megacolon iNOS, C. difficile Toxin A/B and Fidaxomicin).
